The three problems

1. parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

Our tenants write the day first. The parser reads month first, so day and month swap: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and most of its reports. It comes in on every channel: app, email, and the booking-v2 form.

“Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July. Is this a joke.” (R-41200, harbour.dental)

2. hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, month first. The form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The dates swap, but the parser is not involved: fixing the parser leaves this bug in place. It shows up only on the form channel of booking-v3 tenants. The kb’s own table already says it is not this ticket and has no ticket of its own.

“We had two people show up on 8 June. They were booked for 6 August.” (R-41241, alder.physio)

3. one-day move: a date lands one day early

These reports do not swap day and month. The date moves by one day: Tuesday becomes Monday. A flip cannot do that. It reads like a time zone or day-boundary bug: the same meeting shows on different days for clients in different cities. The desk already noted these reports in desk-notes.json.

“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting. We held it twice.” (R-41344, pale.harbour)

Where this page differs from the kb rule. The kb rule (causes.ts) knows only two causes. It files the one-day moves under parser by tenant and footnotes them. This page counts them as a problem of their own, because a parser fix will not fix them. The kb’s table, unchanged:
